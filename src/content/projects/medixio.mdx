## Problem

[placeholder — describe the actual problem you're solving for patients with chronic conditions. Who specifically? What breaks today? What's the moment of failure that motivated this — a missed follow-up, a forgotten medication, a scheduling fall-through?]

Chronic care patients touch 4 to 8 specialists across a year. Each one schedules independently. Medications change. Side-effects need reporting back. The coordination cost lands on the patient at the moment they have least capacity to absorb it.

## Approach

[placeholder — sketch the architecture in two paragraphs. Agent loop, tools, persistence, escalation path. Where does the LLM stop and deterministic code take over?]

The agent is a long-running conversation tied to a patient record. Tools wrap real clinical actions: schedule a follow-up, log a side-effect, reach a human nurse, surface a missed dose. The LLM proposes; a deterministic layer executes. State persists between sessions so a patient can resume mid-thought a week later.
